AI-Driven Automation, Measurements and Control Systems Courses

AI-AMCe is an Erasmus+ project of six European universities. We are developing new exercises for automation, measurement and control (AMC) courses, together with an AI system that recommends exercises to each student based on their prior knowledge, interests and the course goals.

Why AI-AMCe?

Students in AMC courses come from different study programmes, such as mechatronics, mechanical engineering, chemistry, biomedical engineering and economics, and some are international students. Their prior knowledge varies a lot, which makes it hard to teach a course that suits everyone.

In an online survey with more than 220 student and teacher responses, a common complaint was that theory is not well connected to practice and that courses have too few hands-on experiments.
