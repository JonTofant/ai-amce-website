Privacy

This website sets no cookies and does not collect personal data about visitors.

Cookies

The website sets no cookies and stores nothing in your browser. There is no advertising, no social media tracking and no cookie banner, because none is needed.

Visitor statistics

To report how many people the project reaches, as the Erasmus+ grant requires, visits are counted with GoatCounter. GoatCounter records only totals: which pages were viewed, the referring website, the browser and operating system, the screen size, and the country and region derived from the IP address.

GoatCounter does not store IP addresses, the full browser identification or any identifier. To tell a returning visitor from a new one, it keeps a combination of the IP address and browser identification in memory for up to 8 hours and does not write it to disk. GoatCounter is operated by Martin Tournoij in Ireland, and its data is stored with Hetzner Online GmbH in Finland and Germany. See the GoatCounter privacy policy.

Hosting

The website is hosted on GitHub Pages. When you visit a GitHub Pages site, GitHub logs your IP address for security purposes. The project has no access to these logs. See the GitHub privacy statement.

People named or shown on this site

The names of Management Board members and the photo from the kick-off meeting are published with the consent of the people concerned. To have your name or image removed, write to the contact above.

Your rights

Under the General Data Protection Regulation you can ask for access to, correction or deletion of personal data about you, and object to its processing. Write to the contact or the data protection officer above. You can also complain to the Slovenian supervisory authority, the Information Commissioner of the Republic of Slovenia.

Links to other websites

This website links to partner and other external websites. Their own privacy policies apply once you follow a link.
